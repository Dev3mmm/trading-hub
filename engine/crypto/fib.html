Fib OTE Monitor  ← hub

Observation only -- no trading, no money involved. Every ~25 minutes it checks the top liquid coins, decides daily-timeframe bias, finds the matching swing on the 15-minute chart, and when price touches the 0.5–0.68 retracement zone in that direction it logs the signal and saves the actual chart it used — so the swing selection can be checked by eye, not trusted blind. Then it just watches: does price reach the 0.3 target within 8 hours, retrace deeper to 0.382, or break the invalidation level?
Why this exists. The backtested version of this idea (S10-B) looked like it passed, but a cost-accounting bug on the short side made it look better than it was -- once fixed, it does not have a proven edge. This monitor is the honest, no-money way to keep checking the idea live: click any row for the real chart the algorithm drew.
