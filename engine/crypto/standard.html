Analysis standard (v1) and the evidence behind it

Last updated 2026-09-24. Everything below was backtested on Binance USDT-M futures (about 8-9 months of candles, top coins by volume), fees and slippage included, and split into a first and second half to check the result is not a one-off. "R" = one unit of risk (the distance to the stop). Not financial advice.

STANDARD v1 (what the live scanner alerts on)

A setup is STANDARD only if ALL of these are true:

  1. Timeframe: 4h (entry candle timeframe).
  2. Direction: long (buy) only.
  3. Daily trend agrees: daily close above the 50-day EMA and the EMA rising.
  4. Support level: the liquidity that was swept is a real level: touched at least twice before, OR it lines up with a daily swing high/low.
  5. ICT sequence: sweep of that level and close back inside, then a market structure shift, then entry at the FVG / order block zone (limit at the zone midpoint).
  6. Stop beyond the sweep extreme, target at the next liquidity level (at least 1.5R).

Everything else (1h setups, sells, no S/R level, against the daily trend) is still scanned and tracked, but does not send alerts, because it did not show an edge.

Backtest of the closest match (4h, S/R touched 2+ times or at a daily level, daily trend aligned): about +0.17 to +0.19R per trade, positive in both halves of the period, buys about +0.40R. About 130-180 trades. Statistical strength is modest (t about 1.7). It is a lead to be confirmed by the live paper tracker, not a proven edge.

What did work (separate strategy)

Daily trend-following, long only: buy a break of the 20/55/100-day high, exit on a break of the 10/20/30-day low. About +14% to +60% per trade, 38-39% win rate, positive in both halves. Caveats: survivorship bias (today's top coins), the second half was much weaker, holds last 4-11 weeks, and shorts lose.

Rules of use

  • Paper-trade first. The live tracker at http://localhost:8765/ has a "STANDARD v1" row: it needs about 30 closed STANDARD trades before its win rate means anything.
  • Use a stop on every trade. Risk a small fixed amount per trade.
  • Do not trade the news release by hand. The forex board is for awareness and risk (know when volatility hits).
  • Re-run the backtests (backtest.py, sr_backtest.py, ta_lab.py, wade_lab.py, trend_daily.py) before changing the standard. A rule only enters the standard if it stays positive after fees in both halves of the data.
