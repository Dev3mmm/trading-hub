How the paper book decides

Fill: a closed 1-minute candle trades down to the buy limit (gap-through fills at the open). Unfilled after 2 hours = expired.
Fill candle: only the stop is checked there (conservative).
TP1: sells half and moves the stop on the rest to your entry. The rest goes to TP2 if set, otherwise rides to the time exit.
Exits: stop, TP2, time exit 2 hours after the fill, or the news cutoff (open trades closed and unfilled orders cancelled). BTC kill switch cancels unfilled orders.
Money: each trade risks $2 (position = $2 / stop distance). Fee 0.14% round trip. Trades are tracked independently: if all 10 filled at once you would need far more than $100.
