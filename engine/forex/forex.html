Forex News Board  ← hub

High-impact releases: when they hit, whether they came out positive or negative for the currency, and which pairs to long or short.
Strategy: the first 10 minutes. When a number prints, the board marks it positive or negative for the currency and shows what to trade: positive → long that currency, negative → short it (for a pair where it is the quote currency, the pair goes the other way). Trade window = release to +10 minutes, small size, tight stop. Which way "positive" points is learned from history where the evidence is convincing (unemployment and jobless claims are inverted). The paper-trade scoreboard tests entering 1, 2, 3 and 5 minutes after the release so we learn how fast it must be done. Speed matters: the first seconds are gone before any screen updates. Not financial advice.
