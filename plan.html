What "the standard" means

  • 4-hour timeframe, buys only.
  • Daily trend is up: the daily close is above the 50-day average and that average is rising.
  • The level being defended is a real support: touched 2+ times before, or lines up with a daily swing low.
  • The ICT sequence completed: liquidity sweep of that level, then a market structure shift, then a return to the entry zone (FVG / order block).
  • Stop below the sweep, target at the next liquidity level (at least 1.5x the risk).
